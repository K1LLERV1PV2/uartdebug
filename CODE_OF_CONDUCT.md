# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Uart Debug should be a respectful, practical place to discuss software, electronics, documentation, and hardware testing. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate constructively and assume good intent;
- welcome different experience levels and perspectives;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and observable behavior;
- respect privacy, hardware ownership, and safety boundaries;
- accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, and unsafe or unauthorized hardware testing are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents Uart Debug or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [uartdebug@gmail.com](mailto:uartdebug@gmail.com). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal details.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, reject changes, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its participants. Reports will be reviewed as privately and impartially as reasonably possible.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
